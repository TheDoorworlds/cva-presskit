Description

Short:If Vampire Survivors and Stardew Valley had a weird, cat-filled baby, it would be Cats vs Aliens. Build, harvest, recruit, fight, and conquer The Planet in this bullet heaven meets cozy gaming title.

Long:Welcome to The Planet, where cozy chaos is in full swing! Cats vs. Aliens brings the addictive game loops of bullet heavens into cozy gaming. Harvest diverse materials, build a cozy home base, recruit lovingly handcrafted characters (that you can play as), fend off waves of aliens in auto-fire combat, and use upgrades to find synergies that turn players into the most powerful feline The Planet has ever seen. Cats vs Aliens additionally features a unique Cozy Mode system that allows players to switch up the intensity at any moment of play. This system allows players to dynamically choose their experience, allowing anything from the default, challenging experience to a very chill style of play where they become an immortal cat god!

Key Features

  • Gather, Harvest, Build

    Find diverse materials around The Planet, then use them to upgrade your base (and eventually, yourself)!

  • Engage in Quests

    The sapient cat folk you find on The Planet will give you quests! Complete them, and be rewarded.

  • Cozy Mode

    Choose your intensity level! Play with some challenge, or none at all, and change between them dynamically, whenever you want.

  • Recruit New Characters

    Find new characters to recruit to your cause, then play as them when they trust you enough!

About the Studio & Team

Echo in a Void Games, LLC is a single dev based in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. I have a degree in graphic design with a heavy focus on digital art and I focus on making games that are fun and subservise, bringing together disparate types of mechanics to create brand new experiences for players. I create all artwork in my games myself so I can bring my own weird bit of flair to everything.
